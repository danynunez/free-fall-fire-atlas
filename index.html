Distance = √[(ΔO₂/5%)² + (Δp/3 psi)² + (Δflow/10 cm/s)²] to the nearest published test envelope for your selected fuels. A heuristic similarity measure, not a flammability prediction: 0 means each variable falls inside a study's reported range, though those values may not have been tested together. Fuel, thickness, geometry, ignition and gravity are not included.

Data. 19 of 20 records are matched to their NASA Physical Sciences Informatics accession (psi.nasa.gov), via PSI's public investigation index, plus, NASA Technical Reports and the Space Apps 2026 “Flame in Free Fall” brief. Records marked NTRS were checked against technical reports; ≈ marks approximate envelopes. Saffire‑IV–VI comes from NTRS and may not yet be in PSI.
Scope. PSI holds no partial‑gravity combustion data yet (LUCI and FM2 expected). Lunar and Mars behaviour cannot be directly extrapolated from microgravity or 1 g results. Rocket propellant fires are out of scope.
